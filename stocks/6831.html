公司簡介

Taiwan Microloops Corp. engages in the manufacture and sale of thermal management core components, vapor chambers, and heat sinks and pipes in Taiwan and internationally. Its products are used in information technology, data center, 5G mobile, 5G base station, and electric vehicles. The company was founded in 2003 and is based in New Taipei City, Taiwan.
