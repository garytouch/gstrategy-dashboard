公司簡介

Nan Ya Printed Circuit Board Corporation manufactures and sells printed circuit boards (PCBs) in Taiwan, the United States, Mainland China, Korea, and internationally. It offers conventional PCBs that are used in the motherboards of desktops and notebooks, and home electrical appliances; high density connector boards that are used in smartphones, game consoles, GPS, PDAs, automobile appliances, and MP3 players; and soft and hard composite boards used in high-end handheld devices and notebook.
