公司簡介

CoAsia Electronics Corp. provides components for mobile multimedia in Taiwan, rest of Asia, and Europe. The company offers CMOS, 3D, and automotive image sensors; processors, PMICs, and NFC components; memory products, such as DRAM, SSD, eStorage, and MCP; and foundry solutions. It also provides mobile and IT AMOLEDs; and OIS+AF controller and driver ICs for camera modules, and MicroLED driver ICs, as well as micro controller units for remote controllers, and small home and LCD applications.
